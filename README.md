## Hi, I'm Andre! 👋

Software engineer and architect in Germany, fullstack and cloud, running [abi group](https://abigroup.io/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=profile&utm_campaign=readme-wipeair). I started with web development in 2014 and have spent the decade since building backend systems, cloud platforms, and the tooling in between for clients in fintech, real estate, and e-commerce.

What I enjoy most is building a product end to end, from the interface people actually use down to the platform it runs on. The systems I'm happiest with are the boring ones: simple enough to explain to whoever is on call next, and dull enough that nobody has to sit and watch them.

## What I'm building

Most of my open-source work lives under [@abinnovision](https://github.com/abinnovision). [js-commons](https://github.com/abinnovision/js-commons), [react-commons](https://github.com/abinnovision/react-commons), and [nestjs-commons](https://github.com/abinnovision/nestjs-commons) are the shared configs and framework utilities I use across projects, and [actions](https://github.com/abinnovision/actions) covers the CI/CD side.
